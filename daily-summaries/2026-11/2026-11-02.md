# Daily Summary - 2026-11-01

## Key highlights
- JCartu reported that on 4x RTX PRO 6000, stock vLLM 0.32 cannot serve DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017-NVFP4 on SM120; PR 61530, PR 60762 and flashinfer 0.7.2 are required. Tuned (dspark 3 drafts, cudagraph capture 128, gmu 0.95, flashinfer PCIe IPC) it reached 214/525/819/1814 tok/s at C1/C4/C8/C32 vs 242/543/822/1527 for a CSF build; untuned C1 was ~127. b12x moe halved C4/C8, b12x linear crashed, adaptive verification unsupported. KV ~3.75M vs 9.25M tokens. WARMIND abliteration took refusals 92/100 to 0/100. [(jump)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1547563567379906651/1566473772087648287)
- On 4x RTX PRO 6000, JCartu reported his flare exports of DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 decode slower than the published gb202 package (cefb84d2): ~9.3-9.5ms/step vs 7.3ms, ~380 vs ~500 tok/s at C1, and C8 drafting drops to 0.1/round vs 1.7; WARMIND-1017 runs with PR #154. Native expert compression ~4% faster with less KV; collective tuning and GPU order no change. Jon separately reported an export with nvfp4 draft head, fp8 markov head, native expert compression, static collective tuning and 64 compile workers matches gb202 performance.

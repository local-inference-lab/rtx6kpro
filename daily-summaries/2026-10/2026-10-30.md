## Benchmarks and implementation findings
- pangfather reported GLM 5.3 FP8 with tensor parallelism 9: decode was 110 tok/s at dcp=1 with a 200k KV cache, and 66-73 tok/s at dcp=9 with a 1,166,425 KV cache. [(1)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1542910591730192434/1565471496988659804) [(2)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1542910591730192434/1565471700282384465)
- A GLM 5.3 Flash TP2 build was reported slower at 8 concurrent requests, attributed to being capped at c4 so requests above c4 queue. One user chose vLLM for its 1.8M KV pool and to avoid tying up system RAM as hicache. [(1)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1542496172176973936/1565404947720306689) [(2)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1542496172176973936/1565405206340968529)

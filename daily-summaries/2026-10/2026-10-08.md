- CSF/MegaMoE support implemented with a load-time encoder and a patched kernel that decodes compressed weights directly. Reported ~9 GB saving on GB300 and expected ~4 GB on RTX PRO 6000 WS without downloading the full CSF checkpoint; performance reported about 5% lower, with possible kernel work to recover it. An investigation agent is implementing variable MTP for higher concurrency while looking into the slowdown. ## Benchmarks and implementation findings
- GLM-5.3-Flash benchmark on PCIe 4.0: about 200 tokens/s reasoning decode and 8K prefill, per linked benchmark run. [(1)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1471527895439638528/1557318105242271744) [(2)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1471527895439638528/1557319382609428572)
- GLM-5.3-Flash CSF experiment: no quality difference observed between A4 and A16 activations; CSF checkpoint reported byte-identical to qad+spark hybrid. Running CSF with A16 activations and BF16 shared experts fits but loops immediately and was not debugged. [(jump)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1471527895439638528/1557352977872982068)
- In a reported 1-2 day test, CSF-QAD did not show the repeated equals-sign doom loop seen with older NVFP4-Spark, and the vLLM image with LM cache was stable with no random Engine Error failures. [(jump)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1471527895439638528/1557410976419020891)
- CSF checkpoint loading reported at about 6 minutes for weight transfer only and 10-12 minutes for full model load with hot caches. [(jump)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1471527895439638528/1557319935133220916)
- A user reported that on a dual-node C16 system with about 55 GB/s intra-node and 40 GB/s inter-node bandwidth, TP8-DCP4 and PP2-DCP4 reached similar GLM-5.3 times: TP8 was limited by TP communication, PP2 by 2x weight bandwidth. PP2-DCP4 had 2x KV cache and 2x faster prefill than TP8-DCP8, and with MTP3 the setup ran at 620-680 tps on C16. On another Intel-CPU server with worse TP communication, PP2 beat TP8 on C16. [(1)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1542910591730192434/1557547261645422702) [(2)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1542910591730192434/1557557305220931716) [(3)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1542910591730192434/1557558014373007371)
- A user reported reproducing el8's repo results using a 6K WS power-limited to 350W and a GB300 power-limited to 1100W; the WS typically pulled below 300W. Under high concurrency the WS reached about 85C, versus about 70C in the user's gaming PC while serving several concurrent q38fn sessions. [(1)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1516097906967576706/1557476958017683498) [(2)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1516097906967576706/1557542864886562868) [(3)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1516097906967576706/1557477800099971265)
- DGX Station has 252 GB HBM3e VRAM plus 496 GB LPDDR5X usable coherently; a second B300 cannot be added except by connecting another Station. [(1)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1516097906967576706/1557422791639175281) [(2)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1516097906967576706/1557425564439023737)
- Qwen3.8-Flash-Next CSF on RTX PRO 6000 WS TP1 (+200 core, +6000 mem) decodes at about 68-70 tok/s in vLLM versus about 100 tok/s with SGLang. Disabling lmcache/YaRN, lowering KV cache, and PCIe5 x16 changes stayed within about 1%. [(1)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1528331644933767190/1557438473978257539) [(2)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1528331644933767190/1557440344356823111) [(3)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1528331644933767190/1557440988128219137)
- A user identified the root cause of the 16 syncs: WSL2 disables pinned memory, so every UVA buffer degrades to a blocking copy. Setting VLLM_WSL2_ENABLE_PIN_MEMORY=1 made a 20% difference; prior fixes had minimal effect. [(1)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1528331644933767190/1557501586131918898) [(2)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1528331644933767190/1557555516191023128)
- Pi needs models.yml entries streamIdleTimeoutMs, supportsReasoningEffort, thinkingFormat=qwen-chat-template, and qwenTemplateReasoningEffort to send Qwen reasoning content. [(jump)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1528331644933767190/1557556036930641981)
- GLM-5.3-Flash TP2 on 2x RTX PRO 6000 WS (300W, +6000 mem, +1000 core capped at 2400) vs 2x Max-Q 300W: with the core cap, prefill improves +2.4% at 32k and +6.4% at 128k; decode aggregate gains 4.8-17.6% across C1/C8 contexts. A second comparison finds prefill near tie at 32k and decode favoring WS by 2.8-15.4%; C16 only WS reached 744.8 and 666.3 tok/s. [(1)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1542496172176973936/1557435530394865675) [(2)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1542496172176973936/1557513923337527346)

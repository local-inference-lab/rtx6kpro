## Key highlights
- A Flare checkpoint built with --max-concurrency 32 --image-concurrency 4 --mtp-draft-tokens 7 --speculative-token-capacity 128 --speculative-graph-budget 64 --mla-compute-mode bf16 --expert-scale-compression csf --autotune-effort high is reported faster than the gb202 tree on Hugging Face. [(jump)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1547563567379906651/1563890037425180744)
- JCartu shared DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017-abliterated-lossless-CSF with a LiL vLLM/B12X Compose recipe: pinned image fd74019f, CHECKPOINT=csf, TP4, FP8 KV, prefix caching, and disk-backed Engram with resident scales. The recipe needs the full CSF directory layout and about 189GiB RAM for Engram, so it should not be flipped to RAM under the 64GiB container cap. On JCartu's 4x96GB cards, the recipe showed about 9.25M KV tokens. A separate standard matched probe on stock 1017 CSF showed C1 258 / C8 871 / C16 1152 / C32 1545 aggregate tok/s at ctx0. The 0,1,3,2 ordering is topology-specific. [(jump)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1547563567379906651/1563842902776356944)
- JCartu reported that disk-backed Engram runs rarely exceeded 350W, while RAM-backed Engram kept the GPUs busy and drew more power; the system is not capped at 350W. JCartu interpreted this as pointing toward disk gathers rather than a power cap, while noting it does not prove a queue-depth or overlap bug. In a cache-size ladder with 108 requests, three seeds, and random/code inputs at about 250k/500k tokens, fresh-prompt TTFT barely improved; warmed 500k random prompts got 7.5% lower median TTFT going from 8GiB to 16GiB, and 32GiB added only another 1.7%. The Linux reader already uses io_uring [(1)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1562440533497085962/1563840171391057932) [(2)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1562440533497085962/1563846332651675708) [(3)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1562440533497085962/1563871455546970183)

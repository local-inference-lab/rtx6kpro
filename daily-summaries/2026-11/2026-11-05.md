Warm follow-ups resuming a cached prefix failed with 'prepared model reported device error 1' (INVALID_DISTRIBUTION) while cold requests passed, and long agent sessions leaked [gMASK]/tool-call markup. The same results were reported on runtime 0794d5b8 and release-18b16e1. [(jump)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1471527895439638528/1567704696976711731)

## Benchmarks and implementation findings
- WARMIND-1017-FLARE-CSF-gb202 on 4x RTX PRO 6000 Max-Q (285W, no switch, Engram in RAM) decoded 275.4/1056.9/1423.3/1851.1 tok/s at C1/C8/C16/C32 with 20.98M KV; prefill 13,341 (32k) and 13,156 (128k). Topology is dual-socket: GPU0-1 and GPU2-3 NODE, other pairs SYS; collectives tuner stayed within 5% of fastest transport, p2p 30.4 GB/s, GPU3 write 22.6 GB/s on Gen4 x16. Remaining gap vs reference attributed to UPI crossings and 285W cap. [(1)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1547563567379906651/1567768327290364026) [(2)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1547563567379906651/1567776541671432366) [(3)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1547563567379906651/1567782700977229974)
- Fabric benchmark on a pull-friendly host-bridge TP4: no read stalls (p99 8.6us, max 197us), pair 57.2 GB/s at 1.05us, all-to-all read 35.1 GB/s while all-to-all write collapsed to 1.9 GB/s; tuner default within 5%, NCCL 1.12-2.24x slower; optimal pull 1-shot at 2-4 rows and pull 2-shot at 8-192 rows. The author asked whether the clustered 8-block path (0x00060008) uses scatter/push across CTAs, since write-to-all-peers collapses on that fabric. [(1)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1547563567379906651/1567632003795386528) [(2)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1547563567379906651/1567632051669045329)

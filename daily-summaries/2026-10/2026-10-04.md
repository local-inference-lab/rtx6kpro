## Benchmarks and implementation findings
- Running two vLLM instances with time slicing is reported working: Qwen3.8 to Glimmer 30B switch takes about 30s, reverse about 7s; peak usage 91.3GB VRAM and 103GB system RAM, with Qwen3.8 KV cache reduced to about 640k. Separate ports and router/proxy handling are required. [(jump)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1528331644933767190/1555985411430416514)
- The DGX OS kernel statically allocates everything; this gives about 2GB more total RAM, but fragmentation increases and with vLLM (and other running processes) about 4GB more RAM is wasted. The reporter noticed no reliable speed difference, with gains/losses within less than 3%. A proposed vLLM change would allocate weights with 2MB pages, saving a few hundred MB of RAM. [(1)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1517199740444475423/1555945032777994284) [(2)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1517199740444475423/1555945893339992078) [(3)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1517199740444475423/1555945596664160287)

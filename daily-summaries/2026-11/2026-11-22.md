## Benchmarks and implementation findings

Stock scored 116/144 (80.6%), WARMIND 111/144 (77.1%); the paired bootstrap gap was -0.7 to +7.6 points. WARMIND raised low-evidence vulnerable calls from 3/48 to 7/48, while flagging all 48 vulnerable runs; stock missed one.

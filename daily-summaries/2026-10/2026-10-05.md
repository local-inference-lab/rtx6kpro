## Key highlights
- A runnable MiniMax-H3 SM120 image recipe is available with Dockerfile, build.sh, launcher and request drivers for ref2va, t2va and fl2va at 1344x768 with 2, 4 and 8 steps. It bundles three PRs: vllm-omni 8490 (B12X plus SOL_ATTN, opt-in MXFP8/NVFP4, H3 comms and quant), vllm-omni 8491 (rank-device communicator binding) and b12x 482 (var-length attention with per-head block lists).

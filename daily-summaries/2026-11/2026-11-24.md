## Benchmarks and implementation findings
- A cited report described a controlled iso-parameter comparison: a 30B model with the same architecture as a 750B base, identical parameter counts, and only about 10% more total training compute; the looped version showed clear gains. [(jump)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1542910591730192434/1574369697464459295)
- RO=1 appeared to help a P2P-unlocked 8x3090 rig. An agent test outside inference showed significant improvement on PCIe5 and PCIe4 rigs, interpreted as an ordering-penalty fix rather than a bandwidth uplift, likely applying to all TP setups. [(1)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1547563567379906651/1574396398651052054) [(2)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1547563567379906651/1574406520442191973)

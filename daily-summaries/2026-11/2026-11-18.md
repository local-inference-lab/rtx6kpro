## Benchmarks and implementation findings
- Pmot12 reported batch-tiered speculative depth K=5/4/3 with replay skip, cutting wasted high-concurrency verification tokens and giving +27% at C=16 short context. The draft fast path used a 2-term MXFP8 DeepGEMM DGHead, Triton FusedMarkovSampler with 32 mini-CUDA-graphs, and skipped 160 MB greedy draft-logit stores. Small-batch M≤64 dense projections used CuTe-DSL MXFP8 decode GEMMs via a torch.compile-safe custom op; M>64 prefill GEMMs kept full-throughput kernels. [(jump)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1516097906967576706/1572478964264472649)
- Marky reported testing Flare commit c2606aa: GPU time waiting on engrams fell from 12-25% to 3-12%, settling at 3-6%, versus 20% then 12-15% without the patch. [(jump)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1560667258232115220/1572405790424301570)
- Ran compared the DeepSeek V4.1 Flash TP2 EXL3 build with a TrellisMX build: EXL3 decode was 23-28% faster at C1/C4 with the same prefill when both engram tables served from disk; KV was smaller but still supported two 1M-context sessions. [(jump)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1571737092122476574/1572398693087313962)
- Tuna reported Hy4 preview NVFP4 on TP8 at about 4.2k-token prefill and 100 tok/s decode with MTP depth 3; KV cache reached 3M tokens, lower with vision. [(jump)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1567521390515585119/1572381886108667984)

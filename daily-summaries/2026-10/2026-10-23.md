## Key highlights
- On 4x RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell TP4, Flare runtime 5dd67695 vs vLLM karmic-kraken had a 2.26x KV pool (20,927,744 vs 9,248,520 tokens), C1 decode 261.3 vs 249.9 tok/s (+4.6%), and C32 decode 1352.6 vs 1579.2 tok/s (vLLM +16.8%); prefill was 16,395-17,137 vs 17,412-18,615 tok/s.

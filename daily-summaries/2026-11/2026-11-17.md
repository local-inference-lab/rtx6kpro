## Benchmarks and implementation findings
- el8 describes FLARE ignition as compiling graphs and tuning a model to the hardware to produce a downloadable flare core, functionally separate from the FLARE runtime; the runtime can be a single executable using hundreds of MB of RAM rather than gigabytes. [(1)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1560667258232115220/1571976057425236111) [(2)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1560667258232115220/1571976167970574397) [(3)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1560667258232115220/1571976272098107463)
- t3rmina1 reports grammar-constrained JSON response_format pins MTP speculative width to one token per round and forces single-step in flight, effectively disabling speculative decoding for that request; an unconstrained request batched with a constrained one also loses its second in-flight step. Paths cited: helios/src/scheduler.rs:2109 and 1075, docs/mtp-design.md:386. [(jump)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1560667258232115220/1572128484732444693)
- pangfather reports Kimi K3.1 EXL3 2bpw emits fewer broken tokens/typos and has better grammar with top_p 0.9 than top_p 0.95. No runtime, sampler details, or quantitative error rate is supplied. [(1)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1528141816459689984/1572059948848783404) [(2)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1528141816459689984/1572062303569059881)

No inference result is supplied. [(1)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1473975364727476224/1571866640638017648) [(2)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1495397776333471775/1571817340734275666) [(3)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1495397776333471775/1571817378633875459)

At temperature 0, 75/78 transcripts differed between two fresh NVFP4 starts versus 0 for Qwen3-ASR; one output was English and one JSON.

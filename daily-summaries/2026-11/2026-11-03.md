## Benchmarks and implementation findings
- FLARE thinking_token_budget=300 cut a looping maths prompt from about 11s to 2s while keeping the answer correct (measured by JCartu). [(jump)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1560667258232115220/1566883657694576881)
- In Kimi K3 QSRT weights, a quantized attention matrix was identified as a possible cause of accuracy drop; restoring the original matrix costs about 10MB per rank, and a real task was tried with the restored matrix (pangfather). [(jump)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1528141816459689984/1567016009469001838)
- DeepSeek V4.1 Flash checkpoint 1017 under FLARE improved MMLU-Pro from 90.3% to 91.0%, except for the computer-scenes and health sections. [(jump)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1547563567379906651/1566788204659740776)
- On a retimer-based box, phased-cluster8 was not faster than gb202 with SSD Engram offload, alpha 0.6, ctx 0, llm_decode_bench 30s cells: about level at C1, -9.6% at C8, -14.3% at C16, -3.1% at C32 (two runs per revision, reported by doraneko25). [(jump)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1547563567379906651/1566934044342493205)
- On DGX Spark, min_free_kbytes mostly affects KV capacity, not decode. Initial run: disk table with 4 GiB min_free and -28GiB gave 3.8M KV and coding-peak median 78.5, while RAM table gave 59.2/152.0 on 8K and median 67.6. Clean rerun at -16GiB: disk 59.3/151.4/288.8 (C1/C4/C16) with coding-peak 77.6; RAM 60.2/157.1/323.7 with 79.4. User stays on disk because RAM takes too much KV. [(1)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1517199740444475423/1566886817712570612) [(2)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1560963206539644958/1566991934621360213)
- Larger collectives were measured at 190+ Gbps; 100Gbps links sharply degrade tensor parallel. The 200Gbps link is shared across both ports. [(1)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1517199740444475423/1567019325468975126) [(2)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1517199740444475423/1567019670819831910) [(3)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1517199740444475423/1567023583153365046)
- DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 on 2x DGX Spark (256GB unified memory) consumed nearly all memory during an llm_decode_bench run, leaving only 8.5GB and 9.5GB free per unit. [(1)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1517199740444475423/1567037331221778484) [(2)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1517199740444475423/1567049963324575826) [(3)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1517199740444475423/1567050075438448794)
- On 3x Max-Q 300W cards, DS41 Flash .6 BRRR kernels were -5% single-decode vs .3 and decode hit the power cap at 4+ streams; fused decoder .9 vs non-fused was +17.7% at c1, +9.2% at c4, level at c8. The .5-vs-.6 regression was context-length dependent, not power; removing a radix indexer top-k patch gave +6.2% overall and +14.6% on turns over 256K. 4-bit Engram measured -1-4% (reported by pete8359). The report concludes weight edits can remove an obstacle that hides an ability but cannot add an ability the model lacks. ## Active work
- Reported: dsv4.1 can be served at native precision on TP2 by offloading Engrams to SSD and weights to RAM. This approach is very good at concurrency 1, but above c1 it struggles because weights must be shifted in/out of GPU per stream, adding significant time per step; testing continues.

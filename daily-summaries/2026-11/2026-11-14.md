# Daily Summary - 2026-11-13

## Key highlights
- HashSpamJam benchmarked four RTX 6000 Blackwell cards at a 250 W per-card cap, two runs per configuration, comparing a 2200 MHz clock cap plus core offsets against default clocks: decode was 289 vs 308 tok/s at C1 and 1873 vs 1755 tok/s at C32; prefill gains were about 10% at 8K, 16% at 64K, and 16% at 128K. The same tester reported 2400 MHz suitable at 350 W and 2200 MHz at 250 W. [(1)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1547563567379906651/1570717780116906057) [(2)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1547563567379906651/1570854688381739200)
- JCartu compared warmind vs stock DeepSeek 4.1F on the same Flare setup (flare 972e28e4, FlashInfer 6aaa9b47; only weights differ). SimpleQA recall was equal; warmind invented answers on unanswerable SQuAD2 10.8 points more and scored 4.6 points worse on TruthfulQA MC1. A fresh 1,000-question SQuAD2 sample reproduced 37.4% vs 23.8% fabrication; a strict abstain prompt cut warmind to 31.6% but stock stayed ~24%. [(1)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1547563567379906651/1570906525025968130) [(2)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1547563567379906651/1570943089802092655)

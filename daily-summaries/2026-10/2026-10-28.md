## Benchmarks and implementation findings
- On 4x RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell with CPU host bridges and IOMMU off, JCartu reported one peer pair reaching 39.3 GB/s both ways, all-to-all reaching 16.1 GB/s read versus 1.9 GB/s write, and ring traffic reaching 16.9 GB/s read versus 33.5 GB/s write, making the fabric pull-friendly. JCartu also reported push collapsing on root ports on this setup. In all-reduce comparisons, the custom kernel beat NCCL by about 2x at one row, NCCL ring won at 64/128/192 rows (roughly 10-25% faster than the custom kernels), and the FlashInfer tuner landed within 5% of the fastest transport at every size.

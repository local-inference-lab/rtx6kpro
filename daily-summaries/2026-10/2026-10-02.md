# Daily Summary - 2026-10-01

## Key highlights
- On vLLM 0.1.dev21968+g5df66adce (image karmic-kraken-beta-20260930-7ec3133af4492daf), DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash TP4 on 4x RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell wedged when queued requests held all prefill lanes; 40 concurrent ~150k-token prompts wedged 4/4 and 20k wedged 1/3. Root cause: PrefillInterleaveController.begin_step ranks never-scheduled queued requests ahead of running ones and gives them all lanes. A calling-site patch filters waiting requests when no running slot is free; with running=32, waiting=28, GPUs reached 100% and generation resumed.

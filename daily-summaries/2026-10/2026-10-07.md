## Benchmarks and implementation findings
- On a 4x PRO 6000 Xeon, a weak 31 GB/s P2P pair was sidestepped with CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=0,1,3,2 plus the default VLLM_PCIE_DMA_MIN_BYTES=6MB, raising DS4.1 prefill from 18.2k/17.1k to 19.5k/19.3k at 32k/128k. GPU reorder alone with NCCL did nothing; TR/EPYC boxes in the database have flat pairs. [(jump)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1547563567379906651/1556958914476638228)
- VLLM_MXFP8_LM_HEAD=1 on DS4.1 measured +3-4% on a 12-prompt C1 panel and +100k KV, with needle-checksum 100/100 and Estonia 30/30; the flag is not in the DS4.1 profile. [(jump)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1547563567379906651/1556958914476638228)

[(jump)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1547563567379906651/1556958914476638228)
